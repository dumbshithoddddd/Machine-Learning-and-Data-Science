# Shortlisted concepts — Round 1 (20 concepts)

Context for evaluators: Roblox, Nov 2026. 55–80% of sessions are on mobile. Discovery rewards 28-day retention and co-play. Under-16 reach needs Kids/Select eligibility (no free-form drawing/hangouts by default; chat gated by age check, so games must work with pings/emotes only). Small team = 2–5 devs using AI-assisted tools, ~3–4 months to a first public version. Paid random items must disclose odds and are blocked in some regions. Research files: ../research/{steam,roblox,crossplatform}.md and the market report summary (co-op horror/survival rising; simulators falling; anomaly-job horror like Sell Fries!/7 Days Cat-Sitting rising; role-split co-op Backseat Rally emerging; Turbo Soccer car soccer; RIVALS dominates shooters; Volleyball Legends exists in volleyball).

## C01 — Night Desk
**Pitch:** 2–4 friends run a roadside motel; days are simple chores, nights bring procedural anomalies you must spot, report and survive.
**Genre:** Co-op job sim + anomaly horror. **Inspiration:** Sell Fries!, 7 Days Cat-Sitting, Exit 8, Scary Shawarma Kiosk.
**Loop:** Day (3 min): tap-tasks (check-in guests, hand keys, restock vending) earn cash. Night (5 min): an "anomaly deck" draws 4–8 changes per night (a guest with no face, a room number repeating, a painting turning). One player can sit at CCTV (tap camera feeds), others patrol. Spot an anomaly → tap it → pick its category from 4 icons. Correct = it vanishes; wrong = lose a "nerve" pip; missed anomalies escalate into a chase. Survive 5 nights = chapter clear.
**Progression:** Motel wings (each adds a new anomaly set), Anomaly Field Guide (collection log), staff rank, uniforms.
**Controls:** Thumbstick + 1 contextual "Interact/Report" button; CCTV is a tap grid.
**Multiplayer:** Roles (desk/CCTV/patrol), shared nerve pool, revive downed friends. Solo playable with fewer cameras.
**Replayability:** 120+ anomalies × procedural combinations; weekly "mutation" rule (e.g., lights flicker every 30 s) with leaderboard; numbered chapter updates.
**Monetization:** Uniforms, lobby/motel themes, flashlight skins, Plus perk cosmetic, rewarded-video second chance, R15 for adult DevEx.
**Competition:** Sell Fries! (~112K peak, mostly solo, chapter-based), 7 Days Cat-Sitting, licensed Exit 8 on Roblox, many anomaly obbies.

## C02 — Fuse Friends
**Pitch:** Three friends defuse an exploding party cannon — but one can't see, one can't touch, and one only has the manual.
**Genre:** Asymmetric co-op puzzle. **Inspiration:** BOMBANANA! (675K+ demo players, June 2026 Next Fest #1), Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes.
**Loop:** 90-second rounds. A procedurally generated device (wires, dials, buttons, symbols). **Hands** can manipulate it but sees only grey silhouettes; **Eyes** sees it in full colour but can't touch, only point with a laser dot; **Book** reads a randomized rule page but never sees the device. Communication is only via an 8-icon emote wheel, laser pointing, and colour/shape stamps. Roles rotate each round. Success = confetti and stars; failure = harmless confetti blast ragdolls everyone across the room.
**Progression:** Device tiers (party cannon → birthday rocket → robot), sticker book of device modules, streak leaderboards, daily seeded device.
**Controls:** Tap to act; one radial emote wheel; drag laser dot.
**Multiplayer:** Mandatory 3 (2-player mode merges Eyes+Book; matchmaking fills with strangers).
**Replayability:** Procedural modules, rotating roles, escalating tiers, daily seed, speedrun boards.
**Monetization:** Cosmetic blast effects (confetti colours), outfits, cosmetic emote animations (functional pings stay free), device skins, Plus perk.
**Competition:** No known Roblox equivalent (unverified).

## C03 — Raft Rush
**Pitch:** Everyone rides the same wobbly raft and each player controls only one paddle-thruster — steer together or spin into the waterfall.
**Genre:** Physics co-op racing. **Inspiration:** Fart Together (Steam, Oct 2026 Next Fest top 20; a crude Roblox copy was taken down), RV There Yet?, Overcooked.
**Loop:** 2–6 players each sit at a seat on one vehicle; each seat has one hold-button thruster pointing a fixed direction. Navigate a 3–4 minute river course (rapids, logs, crocodiles, waterfalls) through checkpoints. Modes: co-op time trial; 2-raft team race; 6-player "chaos raft".
**Progression:** Unlock vehicles with different thruster layouts (bathtub, shopping-cart rocket, hot-air balloon), course medals, cosmetics, weekly featured course.
**Controls:** One big hold button. That's it. Camera auto-follows.
**Multiplayer:** Coordination is the game; pings for "LEFT!/RIGHT!/STOP!".
**Replayability:** Course variety, vehicle layouts change the coordination puzzle, medal chasing, ghost runs of friends, weekly course.
**Monetization:** Vehicle skins, seat cosmetics, wake trails, course pass (cosmetic), private race lobbies via Plus.
**Competition:** Backseat Rally (role-split rally, 28–55K peak), boat-race games; no known shared-thruster game.

## C04 — Fake or Fortune
**Pitch:** Run an antique shop with friends — inspect every item with your magnifier and UV light, buy the real treasures, and catch the forgers.
**Genre:** Co-op shop sim + detective. **Inspiration:** Probably Stolen (660K+ demo players, ~40K Next Fest peak), Papers, Please.
**Loop:** NPC customers bring items (vase, robot toy, dinosaur fossil). Players inspect with tools (magnifier reveals stamps, UV shows paint, scale weighs) to find "tells" against a reference catalogue; then make an offer. Buy fakes = lose money; reject real treasures = they go to the rival shop. Resell genuine items in the shop for profit. "Forger Night" PvP mode: one player is secretly the forger feeding disguised fakes.
**Progression:** Shop upgrades, tool tiers (faster inspection), a museum of rare genuine finds (earned collection), reputation.
**Controls:** Tap item, tap tool, drag tool over item; offer slider.
**Multiplayer:** Split inspection (one UV, one scale), shared till, vote on big purchases; PvP forger mode.
**Replayability:** Procedural item+tell combinations, rare items, rival shop events.
**Monetization:** Shop themes, tool skins, museum display cases, Plus perk.
**Competition:** Many shop tycoons; no known counterfeit-detection game (unverified).

## C05 — Mom's Home!
**Pitch:** Throw the wildest house party ever — then hide every bit of evidence before Mom walks through the door.
**Genre:** Asymmetric physics party game (3–6 kids vs 1 Parent). **Inspiration:** No Body No Problem! (Oct 2026 Next Fest top 5), Worst Class Ever (#1 Oct Next Fest), Granny.
**Loop:** Phase 1 PARTY (2 min): kids earn Fun Points by physics chaos — knocking over towers of cups, bouncing on sofas, kicking balls into lamps, spilling paint. Phase 2 PANIC (60 s, car pulls into driveway): sweep, stuff mess under rugs, prop broken vases back together, push the dog in front of the stain, hide in plain sight. Phase 3 INSPECTION (3 min): the Parent player walks the house with limited "inspect" taps; each found piece of evidence grounds a kid. Kids can secretly move evidence behind Parent's back. Score = Fun − Evidence found.
**Progression:** New houses (grandma's cottage, school, museum, spaceship), cosmetic outfits, Parent costumes, chaos props unlocked by play.
**Controls:** Thumbstick + Grab/Throw button + Jump; Parent has Inspect button.
**Multiplayer:** Asymmetric 1vN; teamwork hiding; betrayal (a kid can "snitch" for bonus).
**Replayability:** Physics randomness, different houses, player creativity in hiding, role swap.
**Monetization:** Outfits, Parent costumes, chaos-effect cosmetics, house themes, Plus perk.
**Competition:** Granny-type chase games; break-the-house physics games exist; no known party→hide→inspect loop.

## C06 — The Haystack
**Pitch:** A haystack the size of a mountain, 24 golden needles inside — the whole server builds machines to find them.
**Genre:** Social incremental/automation. **Inspiration:** Find the Needle (#4 Oct Next Fest).
**Loop:** Pitchfork hay by hand (tap) → sell hay → build conveyors, sifters, magnets on your plot that pull from the shared pile → the pile visibly shrinks server-wide → whoever's machine finds a needle gets a trophy and server-wide bonus. Weekly new haystack.
**Progression:** Machine tiers, plot decoration, personal needle trophies.
**Controls:** Tap; drag-to-place machines on a grid.
**Multiplayer:** Shared pile, shared progress bar, needle-find announcements.
**Monetization:** Auto-forks, cosmetic machines, capped boosts.
**Competition:** Many tycoons/incrementals; idea is novel but genre is crowded.

## C07 — Tied Together: Summit
**Pitch:** You and your friends are roped together climbing a mountain — one slip and everyone dangles.
**Genre:** Physics co-op climbing. **Inspiration:** PEAK (5M sales in a month), Chained Together (~85.6K peak).
**Loop:** 2–4 players linked by rope. Snap-to-ledge climbing with a stamina bar drained by cold, carried items and hanging teammates. Shared items (pitons, snacks) placed by tap. Anchor button holds a falling friend. Daily seeded mountain; campsites as checkpoints; summit photo.
**Progression:** Altitude records, cosmetics, gear sidegrades, harder peaks.
**Controls:** Thumbstick + Jump/Grab + Anchor.
**Multiplayer:** Tether is shared fate; pings.
**Monetization:** Climbing outfits, rope colours, summit-photo frames, Plus perk.
**Competition:** Many "chained together" obbies on Roblox (2024 wave), SUMMIT (PEAK-like, ~1K peak).

## C08 — Ghost Vlog
**Pitch:** Grab a camera and go ghost hunting with your crew — the scarier your footage, the more views you get.
**Genre:** Co-op horror-comedy expedition. **Inspiration:** Content Warning (6M+ claims), Phasmophobia.
**Loop:** 2–4 vloggers enter a procedurally arranged haunted location (school, mall, ship) for 6 minutes. One holds the camera; the camera tags ghost events in frame (server-side tags, not real video). Get close for better shots but ghosts can grab you. Return to the van; the lobby "theatre" plays a montage of your best tagged moments and scores views/likes. Views buy gear and a bigger channel studio.
**Progression:** Subscriber count, studio upgrades, camera gear, ghost catalogue.
**Controls:** Thumbstick + Record toggle + Interact.
**Multiplayer:** Bait/cameraman roles; rescue grabbed friends.
**Monetization:** Studio decor, camera skins, outfits, Plus perk.
**Competition:** Content-Warning-style Roblox games likely exist (unverified); ghost-hunting games exist.

## C09 — Scribble Spells
**Pitch:** Draw a rune with your finger to cast a spell — circle for a shield, zigzag for lightning — and combine spells with friends.
**Genre:** Co-op wave defence / action. **Inspiration:** YAPYAP (1M sold in 6 weeks), Magicka, gesture games.
**Loop:** 4 apprentices defend a wizard tower against cartoon monster waves (10 min). Draw runes anywhere on screen; accuracy and speed set power. Spells combine across players (water + lightning = storm; fire + wind = firenado). Boss every 5 waves.
**Progression:** Rune book (learn new runes), staffs, robes, tower upgrades.
**Controls:** Thumbstick on left; right side of screen is a drawing pad.
**Multiplayer:** Combos, revive, shared tower HP.
**Monetization:** Robes, staff skins, spell colour effects, Plus perk.
**Competition:** No known strong gesture-casting game on Roblox (unverified).

## C10 — Critter Lineage
**Pitch:** Lead a team of critters through short battles — the survivors raise the next generation, inheriting their strengths and weird mutations.
**Genre:** Roguelite creature tactics + breeding meta. **Inspiration:** Mewgenics (115K peak), Pokémon.
**Loop:** Pick 3 critters → 10-minute run of auto-battles where you place critters and pick upgrades → survivors come home → pair two at the nest to hatch a child that inherits traits + a random mutation → family tree grows.
**Progression:** Mutation collection log, family tree, nest upgrades.
**Controls:** Tap and drag on a small board.
**Multiplayer:** Co-op runs with friends; cross-pair with a friend's critter.
**Monetization:** Nest cosmetics, critter cosmetics, extra nest slots.
**Competition:** Pet sims/gacha TD crowded; inheritance mechanic novel.

## C11 — Lost Signal
**Pitch:** Dive into endless empty pools and malls with your squad, grab strange artifacts, and get out before the thing that hears you does.
**Genre:** Liminal co-op extraction-lite. **Inspiration:** DONT SPLASH, Escape from Duckov, R.E.P.O.
**Loop:** Pick contract → explore procedurally assembled liminal rooms → grab artifacts (carry slows you) → Entity hunts by noise → extract with haul or lose it if everyone is caught → upgrade hub.
**Progression:** Gear tiers, hub rooms, artifact museum.
**Controls:** Thumbstick + Grab + Crouch/quiet.
**Monetization:** Suits, flashlight cosmetics, expedition pass.
**Competition:** DONT SPLASH (~116K peak), many liminal obbies.

## C12 — Mayday Crew
**Pitch:** Your plane is falling apart — the pilot can't see the gauges, the engineer can, and only the navigator knows where the runway is.
**Genre:** Role-split co-op. **Inspiration:** Backseat Rally, IRON NEST, Keep Talking.
**Loop:** Take off → procedural emergencies (engine fire, fuel leak, storms) → coordinate via pings → land → rated outcome.
**Progression:** New aircraft (seaplane, airship, submarine), routes, liveries.
**Controls:** Pilot: tilt/drag stick; engineer: tap panels; navigator: map pings.
**Monetization:** Liveries, aircraft unlocks, crew emotes.
**Competition:** Backseat Rally (rally only).

## C13 — Frontline Fifty
**Pitch:** 50 players, one map, 15 minutes — expand, ally and betray to rule the world.
**Genre:** Mass-player territory strategy. **Inspiration:** OpenFront.io.
**Loop:** Spawn → tap-drag to send % of troops → claim land → build cities/ports → form alliances → war → biggest territory wins.
**Progression:** Season ladder, clan levels, banners.
**Controls:** Tap/drag on a 2D-style map.
**Monetization:** Season pass, clan banners, map skins.
**Competition:** RoWorld (unverified), near-empty genre.

## C14 — Fragile Express
**Pitch:** You're couriers delivering wedding cakes, fish tanks and grandfather clocks through obstacle-filled streets — every bump costs you your tip.
**Genre:** Physics co-op delivery. **Inspiration:** Overcooked, R.E.P.O., How to Fish (physics item as reward), Totally Reliable Delivery Service.
**Loop:** Accept an order → load van → drive (one driver) → carry through hazards (dogs, stairs, revolving doors, sprinklers) → two-person carry for large items → value meter floats above parcel and drops on impact → deliver for tip; flawless = "perfect" bonus and the customer tosses a bonus item.
**Progression:** Van upgrades, new neighbourhoods, courier rank, cosmetic uniforms.
**Controls:** Thumbstick + Grab + Jump; driving with on-screen pedals.
**Multiplayer:** Co-carry, door-holding, driver/carrier roles; versus mode with rival courier teams.
**Monetization:** Van skins, uniforms, parcel wrap cosmetics, Plus perk.
**Competition:** Delivery sims exist on Roblox; physics fragile co-op delivery thin.

## C15 — Toybox Knockout
**Pitch:** Eight players draft toy armies from a shared conveyor, merge three to power up, and the bottom two get knocked out every round.
**Genre:** Auto-battler party. **Inspiration:** Clash Royale Merge Tactics, Fall Guys, TFT.
**Loop:** 30-second draft from shared conveyor → place toys on 4×4 board → watch auto-fight vs another player's board → bottom two eliminated → last player wins (8 min).
**Progression:** Toy collection unlocks (earned), board skins, ranked.
**Controls:** Drag and drop.
**Monetization:** Board skins, toy cosmetics, battle pass.
**Competition:** Thin on Roblox (unverified).

## C16 — Pet Sitters Inc.
**Pitch:** Take jobs looking after chaotic pets in strangers' homes — keep them fed, clean and out of trouble before the owners return.
**Genre:** Cozy chaos caretaking. **Inspiration:** 7 Days Cat-Sitting, Overcooked.
**Loop:** Take a job → timed chores → chaos events (pet escapes, eats a sock) → owner rating → cash + unlocks.
**Progression:** Agency rank, pet log, van cosmetics.
**Monetization:** Rewarded video, home/van cosmetics.
**Competition:** 7 Days Cat-Sitting, Animal Hospital.

## C17 — Wobble Tower
**Pitch:** Your team stacks weird-shaped blocks into a tower before the storm hits — one bad drop and it all comes down.
**Genre:** Physics co-op/versus stacking. **Inspiration:** Tricky Towers, Jenga.
**Loop:** A crane drops random pieces; players take turns rotating/placing; reach the height line before the storm timer; wind/earthquake events. Versus: two teams race.
**Progression:** Piece sets, tower themes, ranked.
**Controls:** Tap to rotate, drag to place.
**Monetization:** Piece skins, themes.
**Competition:** Some stacking games exist (unverified).

## C19 — Statue Shuffle
**Pitch:** At night the museum statues come alive — cause mischief behind the guard's back and freeze the moment the flashlight finds you.
**Genre:** Asymmetric hide-in-plain-sight. **Inspiration:** Worst Class Ever, Be NPC or Die, Red Light Green Light.
**Loop:** Up to 6 players are statues among ~30 NPC statues; fill a shared Mischief meter by pranks (swap paintings, draw moustaches on portraits, move exhibits). The Guard player patrols with a flashlight; statues caught moving in the beam are tagged; Guard has limited "tags" and wrong guesses cost time.
**Progression:** Statue skins (knight, mummy, dinosaur skeleton), museums, guard gadgets.
**Controls:** Thumbstick + Prank button + Freeze/pose button.
**Monetization:** Statue skins, poses, guard costumes.
**Competition:** Be NPC or Die, DISGUISE, In Plain Sight, Prank the Teacher games.

**Loop:** Quick matches to 11; ranked.
**Monetization:** Cosmetics, ball trails.
