# Roblox market: gaps, corrections and early signals (as of 2026-11-13)

**Method and limits.** I used web search only. The session's network blocked WebFetch to ggrecon.com, studiokrew.com and sec.gov, so I could not read full pages or the Q3 2026 shareholder letter. The figures below come from search snippets and summaries of those pages. Tracker sites (RoWatcher, Rotrends, Bloxodes, Profitable) often disagree with each other by 2–5x. RoWatcher's "platform CCU" is its own estimate and runs well below Roblox's official peaks: its "14.9M CCU" is not comparable to the ~47M platform record from 2025. Reddit threads did not come up in search, so community sentiment here comes from DevForum, petitions and press.

Labels: **[Verified-source]** = Roblox official, earnings coverage or established press. **[Tracker]** = third-party CCU or visit tracker. **[Speculation]** = my inference.

## 1. The biggest omission: the platform is shrinking and Roblox is the one choosing winners

- **First drop in DAU from a year earlier.** Q3 2026 (reported Oct 29) averaged 138M DAU, down 9% from 152M in Q3 2025. Hours engaged fell about 15%, bookings fell 12% to $1.697B, and monthly unique payers fell 16% to 30.3M. Revenue still rose 16% to about $1.57B. Q4 guidance is bookings down 10–15%. DAU did rise about 12% from Q2's 123M. **[Verified-source]** https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/roblox-q3-2026-slides-first-user-decline-marks-inflection-point-93CH-4985385 ; https://gaming.net/roblox-q3-revenue-rises-16-as-bookings-fall-12-to-1-7-billion ; https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/30/roblox-rblx-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/
- **The retention-first algorithm is deliberate, and Roblox accepts the cost in spending.** The Q2 letter said the change "intentionally provides more impressions for highly retentive games at the expense of near term monetization." The impression cut fell mostly on games aimed at US under-13 players that lean on short-term monetization. The stock fell about 27% on Jul 31 (roughly $9B). Cross-experience game-pass sales were also disabled. **[Verified-source]** https://kotaku.com/roblox-tanked-9-billion-in-a-single-day-after-the-company-admitted-it-was-directing-fewer-kids-toward-viral-games-that-emphasize-short-term-monetization-2000722002 ; https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/roblox-guided-bookings-down-much-18-and-withdrew-its-full-year-outlook-algorithm-change
- **Roblox is courting older players.** US daily users aged 18–34 grew about 40% year over year, per Q2 coverage. Management says it wants to move away from "clickbait-y, or kind of cash grabby-type games." **[Verified-source]** https://www.gamesradar.com/games/roblox-value-tanks-72-percent-as-viral-hits-dry-up-and-execs-try-to-ditch-clickbait-y-or-kind-of-cash-grabby-type-games-for-babies-and-pursue-an-older-crowd/
- **Age-check coverage.** 64% of global DAU had completed an age check by the end of Q3, including more than 80% of 13–17s. Chat DAU rose 24% from Q2. The Q1 age split of age-checked users was 36% under 13, 38% aged 13–17 and 26% aged 18+. **[Verified-source]** https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/10/62722787/roblox-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript ; https://www.channelnews.com.au/roblox-tightens-safety-rules-as-user-growth-slows-and-forecast-cut/
- **Implication [Speculation].** The audience is shrinking while discovery is being shifted toward retention and older players. A new game is competing for a smaller pool, and Roblox is actively penalising spend-heavy loops aimed at young children.

## 3. Policy and gating the report missed (decision-critical)

- **There is a cost to publish to under-16s.** Reaching Roblox Kids (ages 5–8) or Select (ages 9–15) requires one of two things: a refundable 1,000-Robux fee per game, or a Plus or Premium subscription that has been active for at least 2 months. For group games, the group owner must hold it. The game must also pass an evaluation: **500 unique plays from highly engaged, age-checked users within 60 days.** Until it does, a game is effectively 16+ only. Developers have criticised this as a revenue grab, and some report games stuck at 16+ even after paying. **[Verified-source]** https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select ; https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944
- **Ban on rewarded media feeds (Aug 2026).** Games that combine media feeds, autoplay or infinite scroll with rewards for watching are removed from Kids and Select. This followed Steal an Egg's "Reels" treadmill, where players gained speed by watching AI-generated videos. The game was briefly pulled and returned without the feature. Rewarded video ads are exempt. **[Verified-source]** https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-new-restrictions-on-reward-driven-media-feeds/4829188 ; https://kotaku.com/roblox-cracks-down-on-doomscrolling-games-like-steal-an-egg-that-forces-players-to-watch-reels-while-running-on-a-treadmill-2000727952
- **Unannounced takedowns of crude-humor viral games.** *Fart Together* (13M+ visits, ~14K peak CCU on Nov 7, 2026) went offline days after peaking, and similar titles reportedly vanished. Roblox has given no rule statement. **[Verified-source, cause unconfirmed]** https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/roblox-cracking-down-on-fart-games-as-viral-hit-disappears-3426071/
- **Risk from AI moderation.** Many 2026 DevForum threads report automated takedowns. Examples: an 11-year-old game removed under the "Real-World Sensitive Events" policy; ban waves against outfit games that swept up contributors; even an empty game removed. Developers say appeals get only automated replies. **[Verified-source, anecdotal]** https://devforum.roblox.com/t/robloxs-ai-moderation-deleted-11-years-of-work/4798758 ; https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox%E2%80%99s-automated-moderation-is-becoming-a-creator-safety-issue-not-just-an-appeals-issue/4845031
- **Regulatory pressure on monetization.** In May 2026, advocacy groups (Fairplay, NCOSE) filed an FTC complaint over obscured Robux pricing and manipulative design: countdown timers, limited-time offers and pay-to-progress. **[Verified-source]** https://fortune.com/2026/05/20/exclusive-advocacy-groups-file-complaint-roblox-manipulative-design/
- **Creator economics.** Roblox says creators earned about $1.7B over the trailing 12 months. Third-party coverage puts the top-10 average at about $65.7M and the median at about $1,500. RDC 2026 announced **Roblox Everywhere**: standalone apps on mobile, PC and console on the same revenue share, with wider availability planned for mid-2027. It also announced Roblox Wallet for US creators. **[Verified-source]** https://www.notebookcheck.net/Roblox-is-finally-letting-developers-escape-the-platform-with-Roblox-Everywhere.1402031.0.html ; https://www.techspot.com/news/113855-roblox-top-creators-making-65-million-year-while.html

## 4. Genres and early signals not in the report

- **Anomaly / "spot the difference" horror built on a job loop.** I would argue this is the real pattern behind "cozy chaos caretaking". *Sell Fries!* (Sep 21; fast-food job by day, anomalies from 8pm; ~112K peak CCU, ~64M visits) and *7 Days Cat-Sitting* (chores plus anomalies such as the Tall Man; 24 endings, 21 of them bad) follow the Exit 8 lineage. A licensed *Exit 8* Roblox version launched in Apr 2026 with 8-player co-op. GGRecon notes "a growing number of games that hide a horror twist inside a cosy job." RoWatcher's genre table showed Puzzle as the top-growing genre in September (+39.9%, +52.6%). **[Tracker]** https://progameguides.com/roblox/sell-fries-walkthrough-classes-anomalies/ ; https://progameguides.com/roblox/7-days-cat-sitting-walkthrough/ ; https://www.pcgamer.com/games/horror/a-new-version-of-the-exit-8-releases-on-roblox-to-celebrate-the-upcoming-films-premier/ ; https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-week-in-numbers-2026-w39
- **Anime battlegrounds and dice games.** *Gakuran* was RoWatcher's headline gainer in Aug and Sep 2026; it was briefly removed over its maturity rating. *Anime Dice* was Bloxodes' clearest sustained climber in September, peaking at a 68K daily average on Sep 30. RoWatcher warns that genre fatigue is narrowing the field to a few leaders. **[Tracker]** https://rowatcher.com/news/gakuran-explodes-brainrot-rules-week-of-sept-5-2026 ; https://rowatcher.com/news/why-anime-battlegrounds-are-taking-over-roblox-in-2026
- **Skill-based competitive shooters.** *RIVALS* peaked at about 964K CCU (Apr 2026) and now runs around 180–260K. It monetizes through cosmetics and battle passes, with ELO ranked play and input-based matchmaking that separates mobile players from mouse-and-keyboard players. Competitive shooters are only about 5–8% of CCU. Roblox's fall preview pushes studio shooters such as SuperGaming's *Prime Heroes* and *Showdown*. **[Tracker]** https://rowatcher.com/news/rivals-how-roblox-s-top-shooter-built-a-nearly-million-player-empire ; **[Verified-source]** https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/roblox-fall-games-preview
- **The ragdoll spike skews toward gore.** GGRecon would have placed *Ragdoll Reality* ("blood-splattered physics sim") at #8 but declined to recommend it for kids. Many ragdoll clones launched Oct 13–17. **[Tracker]** Under Kids/Select gating, gore ragdoll games are likely limited to 16+. **[Speculation]**
- **Licensed and IP games.** The Exit 8 tie-in and FIFA's World Cup 2026 hub show IP holders using Roblox for marketing. **[Verified-source]**

## 5. Gameplay conventions

- **"+1 speed per second / step / click"** is an established template from lol games: *Every Second You Get +1 WalkSpeed* has ~331M visits and the *On a Bike* version ~251M. It now gets grafted onto other formats, for example *[W4] +1 Speed Keyboard Tsunami Escape*. **[Tracker]** https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/roblox-but-every-second-you-get-1-speed-4122139330 ; https://rowatcher.com/games/10150259576/w4-1-speed-keyboard-tsunami-escape
- **"Escape Tsunami for X" is a clone template.** There are dozens of variants (cars, fish, anime, countries, brainrots), and most sit under 1K CCU. The original *Escape Tsunami for Brainrots* hit about 5M CCU in January and is around 164K now. Clone titles almost never capture share. **[Tracker]** https://rowatcher.com/games/8562618273/escape-the-tsunami
- **Day/night split with theft or danger at night.** Grow a Garden 2 adds stealing at night, Sell Fries has anomalies at night, and 99 Nights is built on nights. **[Tracker]**
- **Small co-op lobbies of up to 8.** Monster in the Mansion and the Roblox Exit 8 both cap at 8 players. Round-based games typically cycle lobby, intermission and round. Common benchmarks are D1 retention of 20–30%+ and 5–8-minute rounds. These are third-party figures and weakly sourced. **[Tracker/Speculation]**
- **Live-ops events drive CCU spikes.** "Admin Abuse" events (Steal an Egg's first was Aug 22) and Halloween events (MM2 ran Oct 18–Nov 16) drive spikes. RoWatcher cites Deepwoken's content-driven spike as the model for keeping players after an event. KreekCraft and others complain that admin abuse is overused. **[Tracker]** https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-halloween-spike-hits-14-9m-ccu-winners-losers

## 6. Mobile

- Roblox's own docs say **over 55% of sessions are on mobile.** Other sources range from about 70% (RoLearn, early 2026, weak) to 80% (Statista, 2024). GameAnalytics finds 44% of players use both mobile and PC and only 11.55% are mobile-only. Mobile accounts for roughly half of Robux revenue (46% through app stores in 2024). **[Verified-source / Tracker]** https://create.roblox.com/docs/building-and-visuals/ui/cross-platform-design ; https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-key-roblox-and-roblox ; https://www.pocketgamer.biz/80-of-roblox-users-are-on-mobile-contributing-46-of-robux-revenue
- **UX conventions.**
  - Keep buttons out of the bottom-left and bottom-right corners, where Roblox's default controls sit. The dynamic thumbstick covers about 20–25% of the left side of the screen.
  - Thumb zones differ between phone and tablet.
  - Use tap targets of at least 44px and 4 or fewer persistent HUD elements. These two are third-party guidance.
  - Desktop has longer sessions and higher revenue per session. Mobile game-pass conversion is reportedly about 2.4% versus 3.8% on desktop, an unverified figure.

## 7. Community complaints and unmet demand

- **Clones and kit games.** Solo developers flood the charts with kit-built clones, and indie developers say they are the targets. Roblox says discovery demotes look-alike names, thumbnails and place files. https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2026/09/17/the-attack-of-the-roblox-clones/ ; https://gaminghq.eu/2026/10/18/indie-developers-roblox-copycat-games/ **[Verified-source]**
- **Creator pay cuts and fees.** Developers object to the marketplace fee change (Jul 14, 2026; cut to the creator share on flat avatar item sales), the publishing fee or Plus requirement, and the loss of cross-experience pass sales (a change.org petition asks to restore them). **[Verified-source / petitions]**
- **Fatigue with samey games.** KreekCraft (Aug 2026) says many games follow the same formulas, and cites overuse of admin abuse events. **[Tracker: video summary]** https://recapio.com/digest/roblox-is-dying-by-kreekcraft
- **Unmet demand [Speculation from the evidence above].** Three gaps stand out:
  - Fair, cosmetic-monetized skill games: RIVALS shows they can scale, yet they hold only about 5–8% of CCU.
  - Content made for the 13+ and 18+ audience that Roblox is now subsidising through DevEx and discovery.
  - Short, replayable co-op horror with a clear ending, the anomaly and 7-nights format, which fits both mobile session lengths and 28-day retention if updates come in chapters. Sell Fries is already on chapter 2.
